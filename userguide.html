Travel Planner - User Guide

How to plan a trip, day by day - version 1.0.0

Finding your way around

Travel Planner has four main areas, in the sidebar (on a laptop) or the bottom bar (on a phone):

  • Trips - all your trips, split into Domestic and International (India is treated as home). Each trip shows a status: Upcoming, In progress, Completed or Planning.
  • Presets - your reusable Travellers list and reusable Packing presets.
  • Personalize - Appearance (colour themes + light/dark) and Data (backup).
  • About - app info and tips.

Getting started

  1. On Trips, tap New trip.
  2. Give it a name, optionally a destination country, and the start and end dates.
  3. You'll land on the trip, with four sections: Plan, Trip days, Packing, and Itinerary.

Plan: travellers, cities, flights, stays & notes

  • Travellers - pick people from your saved Travellers list, or add a name manually (with an option to also save them to the list for reuse).
  • Cities - tap Add a city, search and pick from the matches (region and country are shown so you choose the right one). Each city gets its own weather.
  • Flights and Accommodation - add your travel legs and stays with dates and times.
  • Notes - free text for reservations, ideas, anything.
  • Attachments - upload tickets, screenshots, or documents (PDF, PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, TXT). Each file is stored locally; tap View to open images or PDFs in your browser, or Download to save to your device. Attachments are included in backups.

Trip days: itinerary + weather together

  • Once dates are set, each day of the trip appears as a card.
  • Add plans for a day in the box at the bottom of its card.
  • If you've added cities, each day shows that day's weather for every city. Tap Refresh weather to fetch the latest.

Forecasts reach about 16 days ahead. For dates further out, you'll see a note until they get closer. Once fetched, weather is cached so it still shows when you're offline.

Packing: lists and presets

  • Start a blank list to get the default categories (Clothes, Toiletries, Documents, Tech, Health/Meds, Misc), or start from a preset.
  • Add items with a quantity, check them off as you pack, and add your own categories.
  • Save as preset turns the current list into a reusable checklist. You'll also find preset management under Presets › Packing lists, where you can build one from scratch.

Itinerary: a shareable summary

The Itinerary section shows a clean, one-page summary of the trip - title, dates, places, travellers, flights, accommodation and the day-by-day plan. Use the Print / Save as PDF button to share it.

Make it yours (Personalize)

  • Appearance - choose from 11 colour themes plus a Custom option, and switch between light and dark.
  • Your choices are remembered on the device.

Backup & your data

  • Everything is stored on your device - no account, no tracking.
  • Go to Personalize › Data to Export a JSON backup, or Import one on another device. Importing replaces what's currently in the app, so it asks you to confirm first.
  • The only thing sent over the network is each city's name/coordinates to Open-Meteo for the forecast.

Install it

Use your browser's Install or Add to Home Screen option to run Travel Planner like a normal app - it opens full-screen and works offline.
