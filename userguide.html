Travel Planner - User Guide

How to plan a trip, day by day - version 1.0.0

Getting started

  1. Open the app and tap New trip.
  2. Give it a name, optionally a destination country, and the start and end dates.
  3. You'll land on the trip, with three sections: Overview, Trip days, and Packing.

Overview: travellers, cities and notes

  • Travellers - add the names of who's coming. Remove any with the × on their chip.
  • Cities - tap Add a city, search for a place and pick it from the list (the region and country are shown so you pick the right one). Each city gets its own weather.
  • Notes - free text for reservations, ideas, anything. Saves as you type.

Trip days: itinerary + weather together

  • Once dates are set, each day of the trip appears as a card.
  • Add plans for a day in the box at the bottom of its card.
  • If you've added cities, each day shows that day's weather for every city. Tap Refresh weather to fetch the latest.

Forecasts reach about 16 days ahead. For dates further out, you'll see a note until they get closer. Once fetched, weather is cached so it still shows when you're offline.

Packing: lists and templates

  • Start a blank list to get the default categories (Clothes, Toiletries, Documents, Tech, Health/Meds, Misc), or start from a template.
  • Add items with a quantity, check them off as you pack, and add your own categories.
  • Save as template turns the current list into a reusable checklist you'll find under the Templates tab and can apply to any future trip.

Backup & your data

  • Everything is stored on your device - no account, no tracking.
  • Use the ⋮ menu (top right) to Export a JSON backup, or Import one on another device. Importing replaces what's currently in the app, so it asks you to confirm first.
  • The only thing sent over the network is each city's name/coordinates to Open-Meteo for the forecast.

Install it

Use your browser's Install or Add to Home Screen option to run Travel Planner like a normal app - it opens full-screen and works offline.
