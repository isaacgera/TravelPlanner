Travel Planner

One-page overview
Category: Home Complexity: Medium Local-first PWA v1.0.1

What it is

A local-first, installable web app for planning family and friends trips: capture the trip, who's coming, the cities you'll visit, a day-by-day plan with weather for each city, and reusable packing checklists.

Who it's for

Anyone organising a trip for a group - a family holiday, a friends' getaway - who wants one simple place to plan days and track packing, on their phone.

Why it's useful

Trip planning is scattered across notes, chats and weather apps. Travel Planner puts the itinerary, the forecast, and the packing list together, so what you'll be doing and the weather you'll get directly inform what you pack.

How it helps day to day

  • Add travellers from a reusable list, and one or more cities (with real weather via the free Open-Meteo service).
  • Log flights, accommodation, notes and file attachments (tickets, screenshots, documents) alongside the trip.
  • Plan each day of the trip with the per-city forecast shown alongside.
  • Build categorised packing lists, check items off, and save lists as reusable presets.
  • Export a shareable itinerary summary (PDF on desktop, JPEG image on mobile).
  • Personalise the look with 11 colour themes (plus custom) and light/dark mode.
  • Works offline once installed; your data stays on your device, with JSON export/import for backup.
